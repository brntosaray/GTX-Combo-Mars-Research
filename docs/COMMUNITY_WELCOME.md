# Welcome to GTX Combo Mars research

Hello everyone, I'm **EMO**. This public project is dedicated to **GTMedia GTX Combo (Amlogic S905X3)** research on Android 9 / Mars, including driver behavior, remote-control input and verified or historically reported repairs.

## Current work
- **EMO MARS FIX V1.4 RC4:** I have completed and successfully used this Windows repair utility with **Mars 3.5.1** on our GTX Combo. The **repair package is not yet hosted in this research repository**; don't treat the documentation as a ready-to-flash download.
- **DVB-S/S2 and DVB-T/T2:** Historical operator tests recorded working configurations and OTA-related regressions. Exact matched binaries/configuration sets require their original test evidence for wider reproducibility.
- **Remote control:** Bluetooth keylayout/colored-button and IR F1 corrections were reported as successful.
- **Signal quality:** Application display scaling was investigated.
- **Still open:** power-off delay, front-panel LEDs and fan behavior, plus independent validation on other GTX boards.

## Join the research
Please share your exact **GTX Combo** model/board revision, Mars version/build date, symptoms, sanitized logs, what you tested, and whether the result is reproducible. **GT Combo and GTX Combo are not automatically firmware-compatible.**

Use **[Issues #1](https://github.com/brntosaray/GTX-Combo-Mars-Research/issues/1)** for an existing collaboration thread; start a new issue for an isolated reproducible bug. Use Discussions for questions, ideas and community updates.
